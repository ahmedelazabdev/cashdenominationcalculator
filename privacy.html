Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

At Cash Denomination Calculator, privacy and non-disclosure of your financial transactions are guaranteed by design.

1. Zero Collection of Financial Data

The cash denomination calculator does not intercept, log, or record monetary quantities, denomination distributions, or coin counts. All operations happen strictly within the memory sandbox of your personal web browser.

2. No Cloud Transmission

No mathematical operations call external APIs or backend databases. Once you close the tab, all inputs and counts are immediately cleared.

3. Local Device Preferences

Only essential UI preferences (like the selected currency code) are saved on your browser's local cache. No profiling or commercial cookies are utilized.

4. Secure Exporting

Printed audit slips and CSV files are rendered on-the-fly directly inside your device memory without ever being sent through external channels.
